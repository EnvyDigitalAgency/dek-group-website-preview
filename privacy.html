Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store and protect the personal information you give us.

Last updated 8 September 2026. This policy applies to DEK Advisory Group Pty Ltd ABN 59 609 136 236, an authorised representative of Betterment Financial Planning Pty Ltd ABN 68 643 374 172, holder of Australian Financial Services Licence No. 525640.

Our commitment

We take your privacy seriously

Financial advice does not work without a full and honest picture of your circumstances. That means you hand us a great deal of sensitive information, and you are entitled to know exactly what happens to it.

Betterment Financial Planning Pty Ltd is subject to the requirements of the Privacy Act 1988 and has adopted the Australian Privacy Principles. We follow those Principles in the collection, use, storage and disposal of your personal information. Betterment has developed a privacy policy for this business based on the Australian Privacy Principles. We will make you aware of that policy at the time we give you our Financial Services Guide, and we will provide a copy of it on request.

The law

The Privacy Act 1988

The Privacy Act 1988 (“Privacy Act”) regulates the handling of personal information about individuals. This includes the collection, use, storage and disclosure of personal information, and access to and correction of that information. The Privacy Act includes:

  1. 13 Australian Privacy Principles that apply to the handling of personal information by most Australian and Norfolk Island Government agencies and some private sector organisations; and
  2. credit reporting provisions that apply to the handling of credit-related personal information that credit providers are permitted to disclose to credit reporting bodies for inclusion on individuals’ credit reports.

This Act is designed to protect the privacy rights of individuals particularly in relation to accessing records of individuals.

What we collect

Privacy Collection Statement

As part of the financial planning process, we need to collect information about you. Where possible we will obtain that information directly from you, but if authorised by you we may also obtain it from other sources such as your employer or accountant. If that information is incomplete or inaccurate, this could affect our ability to fully or properly analyse your needs, objectives and financial situation, so our recommendations may not be completely appropriate or suitable for you.

We are also required under the Anti-Money-Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act (AML/CTF) 2006 to implement client identification processes. We will need you to present identification documents such as passports and driver’s licences in order to meet our obligations.

How it is used

Use and disclosure

We keep your personal information confidential, and only use it in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Some of the ways we may use this information are set out below:

  • Your adviser and Betterment may have access to this information when providing financial advice or services to you;
  • Your adviser may, in the future, disclose information to other financial advisers, brokers and those who are authorised by Betterment to review customers’ needs and circumstances from time to time;
  • Your information may be disclosed to external service suppliers who supply administrative, financial or other services to assist your adviser and the Group in providing financial advice and services to you;
  • Your information may be used to provide ongoing information about opportunities that may be useful or relevant to your financial needs through direct marketing, subject to your ability to opt out, as set out in the Group Privacy Policy; and
  • Your information may be disclosed as required or authorised by law and to anyone authorised by you.

Your control

Protection, access and correction

Your adviser and Betterment will continue to take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification or improper disclosure.

You can request access to the information your adviser or Betterment holds about you at any time to correct or update it, as set out in the Group Privacy Policy. The Group Privacy Policy also contains information about how to make a complaint about a breach of the Australian Privacy Principles. For a copy of the Group’s Privacy Policy you can contact us.

Paper copies. You can request a paper copy of any advice or compliance document by calling us on 0484 362 503. It will be mailed to you free of charge.

If something goes wrong

Privacy complaints

If you have a concern about how your personal information has been handled, contact us first and we will work to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can escalate the matter to the Privacy Commissioner.
