How it works

Four steps, no surprises

Every engagement follows the same path, and you know the cost before anything is prepared.

Get to know each other

A first conversation about your goals, your income, assets, debts, insurance and super. No cost, no obligation.

Agree the scope and fee

We set out what the advice will cover and what it will cost in writing, before any work begins.

Receive your written advice

Your strategy is documented in a Statement of Advice explaining the reasoning, the recommendations and every cost.

Implement and review

We handle the paperwork with the product providers, then review the plan with you as your circumstances change.
