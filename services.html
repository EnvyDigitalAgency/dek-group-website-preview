Our services

Our focus has always been our clients and what is in their best interests. We help you define what you need, then build a strategy to get you there.

The approach

Quality advice is the whole job

We place real weight on keeping current with investment market conditions, and on being a steady influence when things turn uncertain. We also stay across the financial, technical and legislative changes that affect your circumstances, because most of what goes wrong in financial planning goes wrong through inattention rather than bad intent.

As a client of DEK Advisory Group you get professional, ethical and timely advice from a planner who understands your situation, across the four areas below.

01 — Superannuation & retirement

Turning super into an income you can live on

Retirement is the point where decades of contributions have to start doing a different job. The strategy that built your balance is rarely the one that should draw it down. We work through the shift with you, including the tax and Centrelink consequences most people only discover afterwards.

  • Tax effective transition to retirement strategies in the lead up to finishing work
  • Account based pension strategies for retirees
  • Self managed super funds, from establishment and investment strategy through to winding up
  • Personal, corporate, industry and public sector superannuation
  • Annuity strategies designed to be Centrelink friendly and provide a regular income

02 — Wealth creation & investing

An investment strategy that suits this stage of your life

Risk tolerance is not a personality test, it is a practical question about what you need the money to do and when. We document your risk profile with you, then build an asset allocation around it, and we revisit it as your circumstances move.

  • Investment strategies tailored to different stages of your life
  • Managed investments, listed securities and exchange traded products
  • Cash, term deposits and investment bonds
  • Borrowing to invest through home equity and margin lending
  • Budgeting, cash flow and debt management

All investing carries risk, and returns are not guaranteed. Any recommendation we make comes with a written explanation of the risks alongside the expected benefits.

03 — Wealth protection

Cover sized to what your household would actually need

Insurance is the part of a plan people most want to skip and most regret skipping. The question we work through is a concrete one: if income stopped tomorrow, what would your family need, for how long, and what is already covered inside your super.

  • Term life insurance
  • Total and permanent disability cover
  • Trauma insurance
  • Income protection
  • Business insurance
  • Assistance at claim time, when it matters most

How we are paid on insurance. We may receive commission from insurers on policies we arrange. The amount is disclosed to you in your advice document, and the full detail is set out in our Financial Services Guide.

04 — Centrelink, aged care & estate

The decisions families put off because the forms are awful

Age Pension entitlements, aged care costs and estate arrangements all interact with your super and investments, and getting the order wrong can be expensive. We work out where you stand, explain the options in plain terms, and help you get through the paperwork.

  • Centrelink Age Pension eligibility and entitlement, including help completing application forms
  • Veterans' Affairs assistance
  • Aged care decisions and how they are funded
  • Estate planning, so arrangements are tax effective and suitable

DEK Advisory Group is authorised by the Tax Practitioners Board to provide tax (financial) advice services directly related to our financial planning advice. We do not advise on other tax matters, and we do not provide legal services. Where a will or power of attorney needs drafting, we work alongside your solicitor.
