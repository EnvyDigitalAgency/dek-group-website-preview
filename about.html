About DEK Advisory Group

A two person practice in Malvern, where the person who writes your advice is the person you speak to.

Who we are

Small on purpose

DEK Advisory Group is run by Dion and Erica Kratz from Milton Parade in Malvern. Dion writes the advice, Erica runs the practice, and between them they hold the whole picture of every client we look after.

That structure is a deliberate choice rather than a stage we have not grown out of. It means your circumstances do not have to be re-explained, questions get answered by the person who knows the answer, and nothing important falls between two departments.

Our primary focus has been, and always will be, our clients and what is in their best interests. Helping people define what they need and then building a strategy to achieve it is the whole proposition.

How we work

What you can hold us to

Tailored advice

We provide advice built specifically around your needs, by understanding who you are and what you want to achieve. We are committed to putting your needs before our own.

Expert advice

We have extensive experience delivering financial planning solutions, we are qualified within the industry, and we keep developing through ongoing training and education.

Accessible and proactive

We are committed to a lasting relationship with our clients, by being reachable and giving priority access to advice and information so your strategy stays relevant.

Professionalism

We conduct ourselves with dignity and integrity, and show respect and courtesy in our dealings. We act in a compliant manner and adhere to all relevant regulatory and professional requirements.

Privacy and confidentiality

When you give us important personal and financial information, it is stored securely and kept strictly confidential. It is not released to any person or organisation without your prior consent.

Clear on cost

We agree fees with you in writing before any work begins, and every cost is set out in your advice document. Our Financial Services Guide explains how we are paid.
